VERDICT

A real substitute, with two real caveats.

Holds up

  • 40.4% direct domain overlap on a random sample
  • PDL is larger than GOI overall (23.1M vs ~20M)
  • Industry coverage is fairly even (27-55%)
  • Most firmographic fields map cleanly

Doesn't hold up

  • No description field, the one GOI field this thesis's method depends on most
  • Geography is wildly uneven (6-66%)
  • Weakest exactly where GOI is biggest: micro companies

What it means

  • PDL is a defensible substitute for company identity, not yet for the retrieval task itself
  • Scope and a text-sourcing plan are the open questions, not whether PDL is usable at all
