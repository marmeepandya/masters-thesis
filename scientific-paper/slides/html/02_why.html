WHY THIS MATTERS

The thesis runs on data nobody else can reuse.

Every result in this thesis depends on ISTARI's Global Organisation Index. A companion paper needs a dataset other researchers can actually get their hands on.

GOI (this thesis)

~20M companies. Proprietary to ISTARI. Not something a published paper can point readers to.

→

PDL Free Company Dataset

~23M companies. Publicly downloadable. The obvious candidate, if it actually resembles GOI closely enough.

The rest of this deck is the evidence for whether that "if" holds.
