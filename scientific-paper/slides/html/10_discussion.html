FOR DISCUSSION

Three ways forward

1

Scope the PDL corpus to where it's strong

Restrict the paper's PDL-derived sample to well-covered countries and company sizes, and say so explicitly as a limitation.

2

Source descriptions separately

Scrape company websites and generate summaries the same way GOI's own pipeline does, budget this as real added work, not a footnote.

3

Use PDL for identity only

Treat PDL as the public company list, pair it with freshly generated text, rather than expecting it to be a drop-in GOI replacement.

"Given the time left, which of these is worth committing to for the paper?"
